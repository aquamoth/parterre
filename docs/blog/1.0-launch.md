# The git graph I could not live without, now on Windows, Linux and macOS

I am building parterre, a modern git GUI for Windows, Linux and macOS that revolves around your
git repository as a beautiful graph. You can visualize and manipulate all your open worktrees,
branches and tags at once, and you can see your open PRs in the graph too, if you want.

![parterre showing branches, tags, three worktrees and four open pull requests](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/aquamoth/parterre/main/docs/images/1.0/hero-light.png)

## Why I built it

I started using git in 2013, when we finally switched from Subversion at the company I worked
for. Being a Windows developer, I was already a heavy user of TortoiseSVN, and I was thankful
that TortoiseGit made the complicated git commands easy to visualize. It became my trusted
friend for years. Others at the company preferred different tools, and while I tried them out,
I found its tree visualizer a killer feature I could not live without.

Now many years have passed, and I find myself working on multiple platforms. Linux and macOS
are just as prevalent as Windows, and I want the same visualization regardless of which
operating system I am on at the moment.

Another important change in my life, as for many developers around me, is that LLMs do a lot
of the work with me. This means I open a lot of worktrees in parallel, and I can have a bunch of
PRs to address, both my own and my colleagues'. If you have not found your way to
[T3 Code](https://github.com/pingdotgg/t3code) yet, I recommend you check it out. I have
absolutely no affiliation, but it is a great project.

![A worktree's menu in parterre](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/aquamoth/parterre/main/docs/images/1.0/worktrees-light.png)

## Version 1.0

Version 1.0 covers the basic functionality, and I feel it is time to get some feedback from
curious developers while I continue to work on the parts not yet on par with more established
products. I don't expect you to give up your daily driver for parterre just yet, but maybe you
will find it a really nice overview of your work environment. Maybe your AI helper left a dozen
worktrees that you forgot to clean up?

![Dragging nodes in parterre](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/aquamoth/parterre/main/docs/images/1.0/drag-light.gif)

## Try it

parterre is free and open source. Download a version for your platform at
<https://github.com/aquamoth/parterre/releases>, open it on the biggest, messiest repository you
have, and see what the graph shows you.

Then tell me how it went. Show me your graph in the comments, tell me what you reached for and
could not find, or open an issue at <https://github.com/aquamoth/parterre/issues> if your
repository breaks it. That is the feedback I am hoping for with 1.0.

You can follow the project at <https://github.com/aquamoth/parterre>, and if you like where it is
going, a star there helps other developers find it.
